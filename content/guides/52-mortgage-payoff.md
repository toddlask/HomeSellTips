This is not legal or financial advice. Your note and your servicer control the payoff. Read the [CFPB guide to closing](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/close/) and get a written payoff quote before you promise yourself a check. The short notes each cover one situation: an ordinary payoff, a sale before the loan is done, a sale tied to a purchase, and a reverse mortgage.

Selling a house with a mortgage does not require the loan to be at a zero balance on the day you list. It requires the loan to be paid from the sale, or otherwise handled, by the closing the contract names. The figure that matters is the payoff quote, not the principal balance printed on last month's statement.

## What a payoff quote is

Ask the servicer for a payoff quote good through a specific date. The quote should show principal, interest through that date, and any fee the servicer includes. Interest is why a quote expires. A closing a week later can cost more than the quote, and the statement will show the difference. If the quote expires, order another. Do not add a daily amount you invented.

The [net proceeds calculator](/tools/net-proceeds) has a line for the payoff. Type the quote, not a rounded memory. [Seller closing costs: what sellers pay](/tips/seller-closing-costs) explains the other lines that come out before you are paid. Commission you agreed to, credits, and prorations sit beside the payoff. The wire you receive is what remains. [Read the settlement statement when it arrives](/tips/read-the-settlement-statement-when-it-arrives) and match the payoff line to the quote that is still in date.

## Selling before the loan is finished

You can sell before the mortgage is paid off if the sale brings enough to pay the quote, or if you bring the shortage to closing in certified funds the title company will accept. The short note on selling before the loan is paid off is that shortage, spelled out. List price minus costs minus the quote is the first pass. If that pass is negative, you do not have a casual sale. You have a gap to solve in writing before you accept an offer. Do not hope the buyer's price will stretch. Write the gap.

A prepayment clause, if your note has one, belongs in the same conversation with the servicer. Ask whether the quote already includes it. This page will not tell you that your note has no penalty. Read the note or ask the servicer to point at the paragraph.

## Selling one house to buy the next

A sale and a purchase are two closings unless one written contract ties them. The proceeds of the sale are not the down payment on the next house until they arrive and until the purchase contract says how you will pay. If you need the wire from house one to close on house two the same day, say that early to both closing offices. Same-day closings fail when a payoff quote is late or a wire misses a cutoff. Build a day of slack if the contracts allow it.

A bridge loan, a contingent offer, or a rent-back are products and contract terms with their own costs. Get each one in writing. A typical range is useless until a lender or a title company quotes you. The short note on selling to buy another walks the sequencing. The pricing guide's bottom line should include the cash the next house requires, or you will sell well and still miss the purchase.

## A reverse mortgage is still a payoff

A reverse mortgage is a loan. The [CFPB reverse mortgage guide](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/reverse-mortgages/) explains that a home equity conversion mortgage, the most common type, is for homeowners who are 62 or older, and that the amount owed goes up over time rather than down. Selling still requires a number from the servicer. Do not use an old statement, and do not promise heirs a check until that quote is in the file. The short note on a reverse mortgage stays on that product. Read the CFPB page and the loan papers before you list.

## Checklist

- Order a payoff quote good through your target closing date.
- Check the expiration date the day the statement is drafted.
- Type the quote into the net proceeds calculator with your other costs.
- Read the note for any prepayment term and ask the servicer if the quote includes it.
- If the net is negative, write the shortage before you accept an offer.
- Match the payoff line on the settlement statement to the live quote.
- If you are buying next, tell both closing offices whether the sale wire funds the purchase.
- For a reverse mortgage, read the CFPB guide and get the servicer's figure.
- Call the closing office on a known number before any wire, using the closing guide's rule.
- Keep the quote in the closing folder with the contract.

## Worked examples

### A quote that is not the statement balance

Suppose last month's statement shows $180,000 of principal. The payoff quote through the 20th of next month shows $181,150, including interest and a fee the servicer listed. Closing is scheduled for the 20th. Use $181,150 in the net sheet. If closing slips to the 27th and the quote expires on the 20th, order a new quote. Do not add seven days of interest with a rate you guessed. These figures are an example of the gap between a statement and a quote. Your servicer's page is the only balance that counts.

A typical range for a payoff-statement fee, when a servicer charges one, is often small next to the interest, and some servicers do not charge a separate fee. The quote will say. Do not budget a fee you have not seen.

### Not enough to pay the loan

Suppose the quote is $300,000 and your net sheet, after the costs you were quoted, shows $12,000 short of that quote at the price a buyer offered. You can bring $12,000 to closing if you have it, renegotiate the price or the costs, or decline. You cannot ask the title company to "see what happens." The short note on selling before the loan is paid off is this gap. Write it before you spend the weekend on moving boxes.

## Common mistakes

- Using last month's principal balance as the payoff.
- Letting the quote expire and signing the statement anyway.
- Forgetting a prepayment term until the statement shows it.
- Promising the next seller that your sale proceeds will arrive at 9 a.m. when the wire cutoff is earlier.
- Treating a reverse mortgage as if there is nothing to pay off.
- Wiring payoff funds to an email address. Payoff runs through the closing office. Confirm instructions by phone.

## When to call a pro

Call the servicer for the quote and for any line on it you cannot name. Call the title company or closing attorney when the statement does not match the quote. Read the CFPB reverse-mortgage page and call the servicer before you list a house with a reverse mortgage you do not understand. Call a tax advisor if you are short and someone suggests a deed move or a forgiveness of debt. Cancelled debt can have tax consequences this page will not calculate. Publication 523 is about gain on a home. It is not the whole story of a short payoff.

You can order the quote and type it into the calculator yourself. Do that before the first offer, not the morning of closing.

## Questions

### Can I sell if I still owe a mortgage?

Yes, if the closing can pay the quote or you bring the difference. The loan does not have to be at zero on listing day. It has to be satisfied by the closing you sign. Get the quote first so you know which of those two sentences is true.

### Who sends the payoff, me or the title company?

In a typical closing the title company or closing attorney orders and sends the payoff from the sale proceeds. You still review the quote and the statement. Ask who is ordering it so two people do not request two figures.

### What if I am buying the next house the same day?

Tell both offices, leave time for the wire, and do not schedule the purchase earlier than the sale can fund. A contingent contract or a different funding source belongs in writing. Hope is not a funding source.

### Does a reverse mortgage get paid when the house is sold?

Get the servicer's payoff and read the CFPB guide. The amount owed has been growing. The sale is often how that loan is repaid. Do not guess the remainder for your heirs from an old letter. The quote is the document the closing can use.
