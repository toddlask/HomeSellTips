The move starts when the possession date is signed, not when you feel done with the house. Book after that date is real, pack so the first week works, and leave the house in the condition the contract described. The short notes are [When to book movers when selling a house](/tips/book-the-move-after-the-date-is-real), [How to label moving boxes](/tips/label-boxes-by-room), and [When do sellers have to move out](/tips/match-the-move-out-date-to-the-contract).

## A date, then a truck

Do not put a deposit on a truck for a Saturday you only hope to own. [When to book movers when selling a house](/tips/book-the-move-after-the-date-is-real) is the rule. When the contract names the day, measure the doors and the largest piece before you reserve a truck size. [Moving truck size guide](/tips/measure-doors-before-you-book-a-truck) is that measurement. A sofa that will not leave the room is a different problem from a sofa that will not fit the truck. Measure both.

If the closing date moves, move the truck the same day you sign the change. A verbal "we will probably close Friday" is how you pay for two days. The offers guide says to keep date changes in writing. The move is where that writing becomes a reservation number.

## Boxes, a first-week kit, and the mail

Label by room and by what is inside, on more than one face. [How to label moving boxes](/tips/label-boxes-by-room) is the short method. Pack a first-week kitchen box with the tools, the soap, the coffee, and the medication you cannot hunt for. [Moving essentials box](/tips/pack-a-first-week-kitchen-box) is that box. It rides with you, not in the back of a truck you will see tomorrow.

Forward the mail before you leave, and tell the insurer, the bank, and the tax office the date. [How to forward mail when moving](/tips/forward-the-mail-before-you-leave) is the short list. A closing folder that goes to the old address is a folder you will chase. [Keep home sale documents](/tips/carry-the-closing-papers-with-you) belongs in the car with you.

## The last hour in the house

Photograph empty rooms on the last day, including the places you repaired. [Move-out photos](/tips/photograph-empty-rooms-on-the-last-day) is the short note. The pictures are for a later dispute about a scratch or a missing remote, and for your own memory of the condition you left. They are not a substitute for the contract.

Walk once with the key envelope from the closing guide. Utilities should still match the possession date. If you agreed to leave the washer, it is in the room. If you agreed to take the mirror, it is not on the wall. [What stays with the house when you sell](/tips/list-what-stays-before-you-sign) was the time to decide. Moving day is the time to match the decision.

## Checklist

- Wait for a signed possession date before you pay a moving deposit.
- If the date changes in writing, change the reservation the same day.
- Measure doors, stairs, and the largest piece before you choose a truck.
- Label boxes by room on more than one side.
- Pack a first-week box that stays with you.
- Forward the mail and update the accounts that send paper.
- Carry the closing papers. Do not pack the only copy.
- Photograph empty rooms, including repairs, on the last day.
- Match keys, codes, and included items to the contract before you lock up.
- Leave utility timing to the closing checklist so the house is not dark a day early.

## Worked examples

### A local Saturday and a hired crew

Suppose the contract says you deliver possession on Saturday at 5 p.m., and closing is Friday. You need the house empty by late afternoon Saturday, not by Friday morning, unless you want it empty early. A typical range for a local move of a three-bedroom house with a hired crew is often about $800 to $2,500, and stairs, packing service, or a long carry add to the quote. A typical range for a one-day truck rental you drive yourself is often about $50 to $200 before mileage and fuel. Get a written quote that names the hours and the stairs. These ranges are labels, not your invoice.

Book the crew for Saturday only after Friday's closing is the date in the contract. If Friday slips, Saturday's crew is a phone call you make with the signed extension in your hand. A deposit you cannot move is a cost to ask about before you pay it.

### The box that should not be on the truck

Suppose the closing folder, the medication, the pet's papers, and the coffee maker go into a "kitchen" box at the last minute. That box is buried. The first-week box is a single container that rides in your car. A typical range for a few bins and a marker pack is about $20 to $60 if you are buying them new. You may already own them from staging. The cost is small. The mistake is the search in a storage locker at 9 p.m.

## Common mistakes

- Reserving the truck before the possession date is signed.
- Guessing the truck size without measuring the sofa and the stair turn.
- Labeling a box "misc" on the top only.
- Packing the closing papers, passports, or medication onto the truck.
- Forwarding the mail a week after the bills have already been sent to the old house.
- Leaving the house without photos and without the key envelope.

## When to call a pro

Hire movers when the stairs, the weight, or the distance is more than you will do safely with friends. Ask for a written quote, a certificate of insurance, and what the valuation of your goods covers. A typical range is not a promise that your piano is included. Call the truck company if you need a different size after you measure. Call the post office or use its official change-of-address service for the mail forward. Do not pay a third party that emailed you a "forwarding fee" you did not look up yourself.

Call your closing officer if the possession hour and the key handoff are unclear. The moving crew cannot grant you an extra night. Only the contract can.

## Questions

### Can I stay a few days after closing?

Only if the contract says so, and only on the terms it states. A rent-back is a written deal with a daily rate, a deposit, and an end date. Do not plan the extra days and then ask at the table. [When do sellers have to move out](/tips/match-the-move-out-date-to-the-contract) is the short version.

### Should I sell or donate what does not fit?

Decide before the truck day. A pile of "maybe" boxes is how the truck fills with things you will pay to store. List what goes to the new house, what leaves by donation, and what is trash. Donation rules for tax are in IRS instructions for that form, not on this page. Keep the receipt if you plan to ask a preparer.

### What if the buyer wants to move in early?

That is a contract change. Keys, utilities, and insurance all shift if possession shifts. Do not hand over a key because the buyer is in town a day early. Get the change signed, then tell the movers.

### Do the empty-room photos need to be fancy?

No. Level, lit, and complete is enough. One frame per room plus the repairs you finished. [Move-out photos](/tips/photograph-empty-rooms-on-the-last-day) is the method. Store them with the closing folder, not only on a phone you might replace.
