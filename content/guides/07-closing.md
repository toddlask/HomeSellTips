Closing is a file, a phone call, and a set of keys. The order matters because the wire is the step people get wrong, and the date is the step the movers use. The short notes are [Documents needed to sell a house](/tips/build-the-closing-folder-early), [Call the closing office before you wire](/tips/call-before-you-wire), and [Seller closing costs: what sellers pay](/tips/seller-closing-costs).

## The folder, early

Start the folder when the offer is signed, not the night before you sign the deed. Put in the contract, every counter, the disclosure, the payoff quote, your photo identification, and any repair receipts you agreed to show. [Documents needed to sell a house](/tips/build-the-closing-folder-early) is the short list. [Keep home sale documents](/tips/carry-the-closing-papers-with-you) is the reminder to carry the set you still need on closing day, not to leave the only copy in a box on the truck.

Check the names. [Name mismatch on deed](/tips/check-your-name-against-your-id) is a small page with a large delay inside it. If the deed, the contract, and your identification do not match, tell the closing office as soon as you see it. A missing middle initial is their problem to solve only after you have shown them the documents.

## The statement and the wire

When the settlement statement or Closing Disclosure arrives, read it against the contract. [Seller settlement statement explained](/tips/read-the-settlement-statement-when-it-arrives) is the short pass. [Seller closing costs: what sellers pay](/tips/seller-closing-costs) explains the kinds of lines sellers often see. Your statement is the authority for your sale. The [CFPB guide to closing](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/close/) and the [Closing Disclosure guide](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/closing-disclosure/) explain how to read the papers. Payoff, commissions you agreed to, credits, and prorations should match the signed deal. If a line is new, ask before you sign.

Confirm any wire with a phone call to a number you already had. [Call the closing office before you wire](/tips/call-before-you-wire) is the rule. Do not use a number from the email that delivered new instructions. Report a swapped instruction at [FBI IC3](https://www.ic3.gov/). A typical range is the wrong frame here. The loss is the whole wire. The cost of the phone call is nothing.

[IRS Publication 523](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p523) is the federal reading if the sale of a home affects your tax. The closing statement is one of the records that publication tells you to keep. It is not tax advice for your return. A tax advisor applies it to your file.

## The house, the utilities, and the keys

Walk before the final visit so you already know what the buyer will see. [Final walkthrough seller](/tips/walk-again-before-the-final-visit) is that lap. Match the move-out date to the contract. [When do sellers have to move out](/tips/match-the-move-out-date-to-the-contract) is the short note. A truck booked for the wrong day is a negotiation you did not mean to reopen.

Gather keys, remotes, and opener codes in one envelope. [What to leave for new homeowners](/tips/gather-keys-and-openers-in-one-envelope) includes what you are leaving and what you are not. Leave the utility account numbers the buyer will need for a final reading. [Transfer utilities when selling house](/tips/leave-utility-numbers-for-the-final-reading) is the short list. Do not cancel the heat the morning of a winter closing if the contract says the house stays on through that day. Read the date, then call the utility.

## Checklist

- Open the closing folder when the offer is signed.
- File the contract, counters, disclosure, payoff quote, and repair receipts.
- Compare your identification to the names on the deed and the contract.
- Read the settlement statement against the signed deal as soon as it arrives.
- Call the closing office on a known number before you send any wire.
- Walk the house before the buyer's final visit.
- Book the move for the possession date in the contract.
- Put keys, remotes, and codes in one labeled envelope.
- Leave utility account numbers for the final reading, and time the shutoff to the contract.
- Carry the papers you still need, and keep a copy that does not ride on the truck.

## Worked examples

### A statement that does not match the counter

Suppose the signed counter gives the buyer a $4,000 credit and you keep the dining light. The draft statement shows a $6,000 credit and a charge for a home warranty you never agreed to buy. A typical range for a home warranty, when someone does buy one, is often a few hundred dollars to around a thousand. That range does not belong on your statement unless you signed it. Reply in writing the same day. Ask the closing office to trace the $6,000 and the warranty to a document. Do not sign because the appointment is in the morning. [Seller settlement statement explained](/tips/read-the-settlement-statement-when-it-arrives) is the habit: read it when it arrives, not in the lobby.

The payoff line should match a quote that is still inside its good-through date. If the quote expired, ask the servicer for a new one before you treat the proceeds as final. Interest per day is why the quote has a date. Your servicer's figure is the one to use.

### The wire email

Suppose the day before closing you receive an email with new routing instructions and a reason that sounds urgent. Do not reply to the email and do not call the number in the signature. Call the closing office on the number from your original engagement letter or from the statement you already verified. Read them the routing number you were just sent and ask if it is theirs. If it is not, do not send the wire, and file a report at IC3. This example has no dollar range because the safe action costs a phone call and the unsafe action can cost the proceeds.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until the night before to look for the payoff quote or your identification.
- Signing a statement that added a credit or a fee you did not agree to.
- Wiring funds to instructions that arrived only by email.
- Booking the truck for a date the contract does not give you.
- Canceling utilities before the possession day.
- Leaving the only set of garage openers in a kitchen drawer you already emptied.

## When to call a pro

Call the closing officer about any line on the statement you cannot match to the contract, and about any name that does not match your identification. Call your agent or attorney about a last-minute possession change. Call the loan servicer for a fresh payoff if the quote expired. Call your tax advisor before you file, with Publication 523 and the statement in the folder. Call the FBI's IC3 if you sent, or nearly sent, a wire to the wrong place.

You do not need a new advisor to put keys in an envelope or to read the possession date out loud. Do those yourself the day before. The pros are for the money movement, the legal description, and the tax return.

## Questions

### What if the payoff on the statement is higher than I expected?

Ask whether the quote includes interest through the closing date and any fee the servicer listed. Compare it with the written payoff, not with last month's balance. If the quote is stale, get a new one. Do not guess the per-day interest in your head and sign.

### Can I skip the final walk if I lived there yesterday?

Walk anyway if the buyer has a final visit. You want to see the empty rooms and the repairs you agreed to finish before they do. [Final walkthrough seller](/tips/walk-again-before-the-final-visit) is the short lap. Take the photos the moving guide suggests once the rooms are empty.

### Who brings the keys?

You do, unless the contract says an agent will. One envelope, labeled, with the codes written on a card inside. A key in your pocket and a remote in the car is how one of them leaves with you.

### Is the closing statement my tax return?

No. It is a record. Publication 523 explains how a home sale can affect federal tax and which records to keep. The capital-gains guide on this site is the plain-language map. Your preparer does the return.
